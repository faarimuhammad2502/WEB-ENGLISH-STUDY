Pronunciation data attribution

The English pronunciation subset in vocab-pronunciation.js is derived from
open-dict-data/ipa-dict, en_US and en_UK datasets:
https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict

The project provides IPA transcriptions; its English US data is based on a
modified version of cmudict-ipa by @lingz. Four missing specialist terms were
added manually for this site's vocabulary list. The IPA entries are shown as
reference pronunciations; the Indonesian-friendly reading is an approximation.
